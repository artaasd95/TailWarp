# Ten Risk Lenses — Overview

This document introduced the 10-lens risk framework that became the foundation
of TailWarp's risk taxonomy.

**The authoritative sources are now:**

1.  [Risk Categories Framework](../risk-metrics/risk-categories.md) — Complete
    reference with metric descriptions, sources, and links.
2.  [Modeling the Rational Human Trader](../modeling-rational-human-trader.md) —
    Full philosophical discussion and category walkthrough.
3.  [Risk Lenses — Metrics Inventory](RISK-LENSES-METRICS-INVENTORY.md) —
    Codebase implementation status per metric.

## The 10 Lenses

1.  Structural / Ruin Risk
2.  Volatility & Noise (LTF, no-volume FX)
3.  Downside & Tail Risks
4.  Drawdown & Pain
5.  Asymmetry & Convexity
6.  Exposure & Leverage
7.  Liquidity & Market Structure
8.  Behavioral & Decision-Making Risks
9.  Narrative & Information Structure
10. Cross-Asset & Systemic Risks

See the documents above for detailed treatment of each lens.
