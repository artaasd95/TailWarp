# Behavioral & Decision-Making Risks

## Overview

**Lens 8 – Behavioral & Decision-Making** addresses risks arising from human limits and organizational incentives. This lens captures model failures, misaligned incentives, and cognitive biases that lead to systematic underestimation of tail risk.

## Key Risk Metrics

- **Model Risk:** Risk that the model used for pricing or risk management is wrong or mis-specified.
- **Agency Risk / Moral Hazard:** Incentive misalignment where participants participate in upside but are insulated from downside.
- **Lucretius Fallacy:** Assuming the worst historical event is the worst possible event.
- **Knightian Uncertainty:** Situations where risks cannot be assigned objective probabilities; fundamentally unmodelable.
- **Look-Ahead Bias / Data Snooping:** Inadvertently using future information in backtests.
- **Overconfidence & Herding Metrics:** Indicators of excessive optimism and correlated behavior across agents.
