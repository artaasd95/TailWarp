# Exposure & Leverage Categories

## Overview

**Lens 6 – Exposure & Leverage** addresses how much capital is committed and to what factors. This lens tracks both overt leverage and hidden factor concentrations that amplify risk.

## Key Risk Metrics

- **Leverage Cycles (Systemic):** System-wide fluctuations in allowable leverage and margin over time.
- **Gross & Net Exposure:** Gross = longs + shorts; Net = longs − shorts (as % of capital).
- **Leverage Ratio (Notional / Equity):** Ratio of notional exposure to equity determining proximity to margin.
- **Beta & Factor Loadings:** Sensitivities to market or style factors (value, size, carry, etc.).
- **Concentration Risk:** Share of capital in single assets, sectors, or risk clusters.
