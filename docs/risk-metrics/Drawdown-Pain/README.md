# Drawdown & "Pain" Categories

## Overview

**Lens 4 – Drawdown & "Pain"** addresses the psychological and capital reality of being underwater. This lens captures the experience of equity drawdowns—the practical pain of sustained losses and recovery times.

## Key Risk Metrics

- **Maximum Drawdown (MDD):** Largest peak-to-trough decline in equity.
- **Average Drawdown & Drawdown Duration:** Mean size and mean time spent under high-water mark.
- **Ulcer Index:** Root-mean-square of percentage drawdowns; emphasizes both depth and duration of downside.
- **Calmar Ratio:** Annualized return divided by maximum drawdown; simple risk-adjusted return metric.
- **Sterling Ratio:** Return divided by average annual drawdown; less sensitive to a single crash than Calmar.
- **Pain Index / Integrated Drawdown:** Integral of drawdown over time; measures total "suffering."
- **Recovery Factor / Recovery Time:** Average or maximum time to return to a new equity high after a drawdown.
