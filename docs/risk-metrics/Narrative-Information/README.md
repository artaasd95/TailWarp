# Narrative & Information-Structure Categories

## Overview

**Lens 9 – Narrative & Information-Structure** focuses on the divergence between story and data. This lens captures information asymmetries and how narratives can diverge from objective market conditions, creating mispricing opportunities and systemic fragility.

## Key Risk Metrics

- **Information Asymmetry:** Some agents have better or faster information than others.
- **Latency & Tick-Time Arbitrage:** Faster participants exploiting slower ones via speed or superior data.
- **Sentiment Analysis / NLP-Based Scores:** Quantitative indicators of tone from news and social media.
- **Echo Chamber Effect:** Metrics detecting circular information flow rather than novel, independent signals.
- **Narrative–Price Divergence Indicators:** Comparing implied narratives (central-bank communications, news) to market pricing.
