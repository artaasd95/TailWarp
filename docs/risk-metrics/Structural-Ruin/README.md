# Structural / Ruin Risk

## Overview

**Lens 1 – Structural / Ruin Risk** focuses on survival, absorbing barriers, and the probability of system termination. This lens is fundamental to understanding whether a strategy or portfolio can withstand repeated adverse events and maintain solvency.

## Key Risk Metrics

- **Risk of Ruin (RoR):** Probability of hitting capital zero or a pre-defined absorbing barrier (e.g., margin call) over a horizon.
- **Absorbing Barriers / Ruin State:** Hard constraints that end the game (e.g., portfolio liquidation, regulatory shutdown).
- **Survival Probability:** The likelihood of an entity or strategy persisting over N periods under stochastic shocks.
- **Solvency Distance / Distance to Ruin:** How many CVaR-scale losses you can take before hitting the ruin barrier.
